Route picker speed on one laptop: what it missed, and what a longer keep-alive would have changed, 25 November 2026
Picker qwen2.5-coder:7b (instruction 1) on Ollama 0.40.0; 14.9 GiB memory (Ollama's "system memory" log line, 141 of 141 loads).
Data: the shadow log (Claude Code first turns through the gate, classified, never served) and the decision log, 20-25 Nov 2026,
read on 25 Nov 2026; Ollama's own log of model loads since 21 Nov 2026. Aggregates only: counts, quantiles and dates.

    cold 50, chose 38, skipped 29, timeout 8, busy 6
    asked 96 times, named a route: 38/96 = 39.6% (95% interval 30.4-49.6%)

    chose    36/110 = 32.7% (95% interval 24.7-41.9%)   <- the baseline: first turns that got the picker's route in time
    skipped  17/110 = 15.5% (95% interval 9.9-23.4%): 15 over 500 characters, 2 with no text
    busy      6/110 = 5.5% (95% interval 2.5-11.4%)

  Why it was unloaded: no cold first turn since the 23 Nov restart had a picker call or a load of its model in the
    5 minutes before it (0/47, 95% interval 0.0-7.6%). Five minutes is Ollama's default keep-alive, and on 25 Nov
    /api/ps listed the loaded model with an expiry 5 minutes after the call that had just used it. So that timer
    alone explains every cold turn; none needs another cause, such as another model pushing it out.

After: an estimate, not measured
  Replay of the logged times with a keep-alive of K minutes after each call (route_picker.keep_alive_minutes: K).
  Cold first turns since the 23 Nov restart that would have found the model loaded (n = 47):
    K = 15: 15/47 = 31.9% (95% interval 20.4-46.2%)
    K = 30: 26/47 = 55.3% (95% interval 41.2-68.6%)
    K = 60: 35/47 = 74.5% (95% interval 60.5-84.7%)
  Hours the model would have stayed loaded (5.9 GB in memory, the size Ollama's /api/ps listed on 25 Nov):
              23 Nov (from the restart, 13.1 h)   24 Nov (24.0 h)   25 Nov (21.4 h logged)
    K =  5     0.8 h                                2.8 h             2.2 h     <- today's default, from the same replay
    K = 15     2.1 h                                6.0 h             5.1 h
    K = 30     3.7 h                                9.1 h             7.0 h
    K = 60     6.3 h                               12.3 h             8.4 h
  If a first turn that found the model loaded did as well as the warm turns did (36/40 in time), first turns with the
  picker's route in time would go from 36/110 (32.7%) to about 49 (45%) at K = 15, 59 (54%) at K = 30 and 68 (61%)
  at K = 60. That stacks a second assumption on the replay, so it gets no interval.
  What the code does, shown on the stand-in (tests/test_picker_keep_alive.py; its times measure nothing): without
  the setting, a second call after the server's idle time is cold; with it, the same call is warm and the server
  loaded the model once.
  This after is not measured. A measured after needs the setting on the live router and a few days of shadow lines,
  which eval-routes --log now splits by the limits in force.

Method
  Counts: trioditis eval-routes --log on copies of the two logs, and a short script over the same copies that
  splits the shadow lines by the restart on 23 Nov (the service journal dates it; the miss times confirm it: every
  cold or timeout line before it ended at 2.50-2.52 s, every one after at 5.00-5.02 s). Shares carry a Wilson 95%
  interval (trioditis_metrics.stats.wilson). Ollama loads are its "starting llama-server" line for the picker's
  model file and the "llama-server started in" line that follows.
  Replay: a moment of use is a load of the picker's model in Ollama's log, or a shadow or decision line whose
  picker was asked (written when the call ended). A cold first turn's call started at the line's time minus its
  late answer's duration. It counts as warm at K minutes when some moment of use lies in the K minutes before that
  start. Hours loaded are the union of the K-minute windows after each moment of use, cut at midnight.

Limits
  One laptop, one model (qwen2.5-coder:7b), one Ollama version, one person's sessions over 20-25 Nov; the counts
  are small (n = 47 cold turns), as the intervals show. The replay assumes the same traffic with the setting on,
  that each call restarts the model's timer (the FAQ's reading, not checked here), that nothing else unloads the
  model (another model's load could, on a machine this full), and that the embeddings model loads on its own
  (nomic-embed-text, 72 loads of its own in the same days, not counted). Uses of the model that wrote no line (a
  warm call from another program) are invisible to it, so it can only undercount warm turns and hours loaded.
  The 5-minute row comes from the same replay, not from Ollama. A measured after waits for the live check: the
  setting on the live router, then a fresh eval-routes --log.
